Calorise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Calorise is a nutrition tracking app: you log meals, see daily targets and charts, and get meal suggestions. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Calorise app on Google Play.

Who we are

Calorise is operated by Sirinat Peewisezkuldech, Bangkok, Thailand (the "controller").
Contact: sirinat.pw@gmail.com

Who can use Calorise

Calorise is for adults aged 18 or older. When you open the app, it asks for your date of birth only to check your age. Your date of birth is not stored or sent anywhere. If you are under 18, you cannot use the app, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used Calorise, contact us and we will delete any related data.

You can use Calorise without an account. In that case, your meals and body details stay on your device. Photo scanning needs an account.

Diet and body measurements are health-related information. We handle them only to provide the features you use, and only with your consent, which you give by entering them. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data (see below).

Photo scanning and AI

When you scan a meal, the app reduces the photo size and sends it to our server. The server forwards it to Google's Gemini API, which returns an estimate of the foods and nutrients. We do not store the photo or the estimate on our server; the estimate is returned to your phone, where you can edit it before saving. The AI provider processes the image under its own terms and privacy policy. AI estimates can be wrong, so please check the numbers before saving.

Who receives information

  • Supabase – account sign-in and the scan counter (hosting provider).
  • Google – Google sign-in, and the Gemini API that analyses meal photos.
  • Google Play – distribution of the app and payment for subscriptions. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • RevenueCat – confirms Calorise Pro purchases with Google Play and tells the app and our server whether Pro is active. It receives your account ID and purchase receipts, not your meals, body details or photos.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising, and Calorise does not contain ads or advertising or analytics trackers.

Subscriptions

Calorise Pro is an optional paid subscription. Google Play handles the payment, renewals and cancellations. To link Pro to your account, the app identifies you to RevenueCat using your Calorise account ID (a random code, not your email). RevenueCat checks your purchase with Google Play and our server stores only whether Pro is currently active. You need an account to subscribe. See the Terms of Use for billing details.

How long we keep information

  • Meals and body details stay on your device until you delete them, delete your account, or uninstall the app.
  • Account information and your Pro status are kept until you delete your account.
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google's policies, for example for tax and refunds.
  • Photos are not kept by us.

Delete your account and data

You can delete everything yourself:

  1. Open Calorise and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and data and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account and scan records from our server, deletes your subscription record at RevenueCat, and erases the meals and body details stored on your device.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Calorise Pro subscription. Google Play bills it, so cancel it first in the Google Play app: Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

If you can no longer open the app, email sirinat.pw@gmail.com from the address used for your account with the subject "Delete my Calorise account". We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email. If you only used the app without an account, uninstalling it removes your data from your device.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, withdraw your consent, or object to how it is handled. Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act, you may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee. Email us at sirinat.pw@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Security and international transfers

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit, and our database uses access rules so that signed-in users can only reach their own records. No system is perfectly secure. Our service providers may process information on servers outside Thailand.

Not medical advice

Calorise gives general nutrition estimates and suggestions for healthy adults. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before changing your diet, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating. If you feel unwell, seek professional help.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
