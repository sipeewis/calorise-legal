01 · The bean

One hillside. Every cup.

Every cup here comes from one slope in Doi Pangkhon, Chiang Rai, 1,400 metres up. The cherries are picked ripe in January, washed, and fermented for thirty-six hours before they dry on raised beds.

We roast once a week, on Tuesday morning, and rest the beans for nine days before they reach the grinder. Nothing else goes in the hopper. There is no house blend, because there is no house.

Doi Pangkhon, Chiang Rai · Washed · Picked in January

Two sips, and you know the whole cup.

The first sip is brown sugar and tamarind. The second, once it cools a little, is black tea. That is the coffee. We have spent two years making sure nothing else gets in its way.

It comes in a 150 ml stoneware cup thrown in Lampang, with milk if you ask for it. There is no large. There is no oat. There is one table, and it seats eight.

Lampang stoneware · 150 ml · Milk on request
